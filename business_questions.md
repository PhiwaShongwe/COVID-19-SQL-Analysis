# Business questions

1. Which countries had the highest share of their population infected?
2. Which continents recorded the most deaths?
3. What percentage of confirmed cases ended in death, by country?
4. How did global cases and deaths change month by month?
5. How did cumulative vaccinations grow in selected countries?
6. Did countries with higher vaccination coverage have different outcomes?

**Data:** Our World in Data COVID-19 (85,171 rows, 2020-01-01 to 2021-04-30, 210 countries).
**Stack:** PostgreSQL, pgAdmin 4, Tableau. Bronze (raw) > Silver (clean) > Gold (analysis-ready).
